# Cat Tree

They chewed through his fence. They took Biscuit. A gritty 8-bit, top-down browser game: three dark forests, a beaver syndicate, one water gun.

## Play
Download the repository (Code › Download ZIP), unzip it, double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser. No install needed.
The original cute version is `classic.html`.

- Arrows / WASD: move (or hold a finger/mouse on the screen to walk toward it)
- Space: jump over fallen trees, logs and running beavers
- X (or J / F): shoot the water gun (find it early in level 1; the tank refills when you stop shooting)
- Enter: start, continue · M: sound on/off · P: pause

## Levels
I. The Pines (dusk, mist) · II. Rot Hollow (rain) · III. The Dam (storm, lightning). Biscuit is caged on the dam.

## The Dam Syndicate
- **Gnawer** (leather jacket): fells trees across the path, straight or diagonal. Watch for the red warning line.
- **Lumberjack** (flannel, beanie): chews faster, bigger trees.
- **Speedy** (tracksuit): zig-zags, doubles back, lunges at you, then bolts.
- **Roller** (hard hat): rolls logs down at you, speeding up, slowing down and surging.

Soak a beaver and it runs off dripping. Soak a Gnawer before the tree falls and the tree stays up. Three hearts per level; lose them all and the level starts again.
